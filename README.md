## 🧬 ABOUT THIS PROJECT

<img align="right" src="https://i.imgur.com/3e5Ujxz.gif" width="420"/>

**Mewgenics** is a deeply tactical legacy roguelite from **Edmund McMillen** *(The Binding of Isaac, Super Meat Boy)* and **Tyler Glaiel** *(Closure, The End Is Nigh)*.

Welcome to **Boon County** — where each day you assemble a team from your ever-growing litter, outfit your cats with class-specific collars, and send your mutant feline squad clawing through turn-based battles that demand careful positioning, wild power combos, and environmental shenanigans.

Cats that return home carry **scars, experience — and sometimes extra heads.** Pass down mutations, tinker with bloodlines, and build ever stranger, more powerful cats for the next adventure.
